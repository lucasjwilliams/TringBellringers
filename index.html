Learn to ring About the bells

Music, teamwork and tradition in the heart of Tring

We are the bellringers of St Peter & St Paul’s Church, Tring. We ring for weddings, civic occasions, national events and, most importantly, for fun! We are always pleased to welcome new ringers and visitors.

Bellringing is a unique mix of music, rhythm, teamwork and tradition. It is sociable, absorbing and surprisingly challenging, and you do not need to be musical or experienced to learn.

If you are curious, come and see what happens in the tower.

When we ring

  • Tuesday 19:00 – 20:00 Learners’ session
  • Tuesday 20:00 – 21:15 General practice
  • Sunday 09:30 – 10:00 Morning ringing

Visitors are always welcome. Please get in touch before visiting, as arrangements can occasionally vary.
